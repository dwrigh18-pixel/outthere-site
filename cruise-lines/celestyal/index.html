What this number means

It counts members of Out There Together who have shortlisted or booked at least one upcoming Celestyal sailing. Each person is counted once. It does not count every LGBTQ+ person sailing with Celestyal, so the real number on board will be higher.

We do not rate cruise lines. This page reports what our members are doing, not how welcoming a cruise line is. Out There Together opened to the public in October 2026, so the totals are small and growing. See every cruise line's total.

Frequently asked questions

Are there LGBTQ+ travellers on Celestyal?

Yes. 1 Out There Together member has shortlisted or booked an upcoming Celestyal sailing, as of 25 November 2026. That only counts members of the app, so the real number is higher.

How do I meet other LGBTQ+ travellers on a Celestyal sailing?

Find your Celestyal sailing in the Out There Together app, then add it to your shortlist or mark it Booked. Verified members can see who else is on the same sailing, say hello in the sailing chat and plan meetups before boarding.

Can I book a Celestyal sailing through Out There Together?

No. Out There Together is a social app, not a travel agent. When you are ready to book, we point you to the cruise line or an authorised agent, and some of those partners pay us a referral fee. Details are in our affiliate disclosure.

Does this page show who is on which sailing?

No. Only the total for the cruise line is public. Names, photos, profiles and the sailing each person is on stay inside the app, where you choose what is visible.
