How we count

A member is counted for a cruise line when they have shortlisted or booked at least one upcoming sailing with that line. Sailings that have already returned are not counted. We publish the total for each cruise line and nothing else: never a name, a photo, a profile or the sailing someone is on. The privacy policy explains this in full.

Frequently asked questions

Which cruise lines have a big LGBTQ+ following?

The surest answer is the companies that run sailings for LGBTQ+ travellers: Atlantis Events, Diva Destinations, Olivia Travel and VACAYA. Among the mainstream lines, Out There Together members have most often shortlisted or booked sailings with MSC Cruises, Norwegian Cruise Line and Carnival Cruise Line. Our totals are still small, so treat them as an early signal.

How do I find other LGBTQ+ people on my cruise?

Find your sailing in the Out There Together app, then add it to your shortlist or mark it Booked. Verified members can see who else is on the same sailing, say hello in the sailing chat and plan meetups before anyone boards.

Do you sell cruises?

No. Out There Together is a social app, not a travel agent. When you are ready to book, we point you to the cruise line or an authorised agent, and some of those partners pay us a referral fee. Details are in our affiliate disclosure.

Can anyone see that I am on a sailing?

Not from this page. Only cruise line totals are public. Inside the app, you choose what is visible, and only verified members can see who is on a sailing.
