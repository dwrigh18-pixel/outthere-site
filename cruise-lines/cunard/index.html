Ports where same-sex relationships are against the law

Some itineraries call at countries where same-sex relationships are a criminal offence or are prosecuted. We count them so you know before you book, not after.

12%of upcoming Cunard sailings with an itinerary in the app call at one of these ports.

The countries are Malaysia, Morocco, Jamaica, Grenada, Tunisia and Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, with the most visited first.

Each name links to the Wikipedia article our guidance is drawn from, last checked 30 September 2026. Laws change, so read the article before you travel.

This counts the itinerary. It says nothing about the policies of Cunard or how you are treated on board. In the app, every port on your sailing carries its own guidance.

What this number means

It counts members of Out There Together who have shortlisted or booked at least one upcoming Cunard sailing. Each person is counted once. It does not count every LGBTQ+ person sailing with Cunard, so the real number on board will be higher.

We do not rate cruise lines. This page reports what our members are doing, not how welcoming a cruise line is. Out There Together opened to the public in October 2026, so the totals are small and growing. See every cruise line's total.

Frequently asked questions

Are there LGBTQ+ travellers on Cunard?

No Out There Together member has marked an upcoming Cunard sailing yet, as of 26 November 2026. That only reflects members of the app, which opened to the public in October 2026. It says nothing about who sails with Cunard.

How do I meet other LGBTQ+ travellers on a Cunard sailing?

Find your Cunard sailing in the Out There Together app, then add it to your shortlist or mark it Booked. Verified members can see who else is on the same sailing, say hello in the sailing chat and plan meetups before boarding.

Can I book a Cunard sailing through Out There Together?

No. Out There Together is a social app, not a travel agent. When you are ready to book, we point you to the cruise line or an authorised agent, and some of those partners pay us a referral fee. Details are in our affiliate disclosure.

Does this page show who is on which sailing?

No. Only the total for the cruise line is public. Names, photos, profiles and the sailing each person is on stay inside the app, where you choose what is visible.
