What this number means

It counts members of Out There Together who have shortlisted or booked at least one upcoming Marella Cruises sailing. Each person is counted once. It does not count every LGBTQ+ person sailing with Marella Cruises, so the real number on board will be higher.

We do not rate cruise lines. This page reports what our members are doing, not how welcoming a cruise line is. Out There Together opened to the public in October 2026, so the totals are small and growing. See every cruise line's total.

Frequently asked questions

Are there LGBTQ+ travellers on Marella Cruises?

No Out There Together member has marked an upcoming Marella Cruises sailing yet, as of 25 November 2026. That only reflects members of the app, which opened to the public in October 2026. It says nothing about who sails with Marella Cruises.

How do I meet other LGBTQ+ travellers on a Marella Cruises sailing?

Find your Marella Cruises sailing in the Out There Together app, then add it to your shortlist or mark it Booked. Verified members can see who else is on the same sailing, say hello in the sailing chat and plan meetups before boarding.

Can I book a Marella Cruises sailing through Out There Together?

No. Out There Together is a social app, not a travel agent. When you are ready to book, we point you to the cruise line or an authorised agent, and some of those partners pay us a referral fee. Details are in our affiliate disclosure.

Does this page show who is on which sailing?

No. Only the total for the cruise line is public. Names, photos, profiles and the sailing each person is on stay inside the app, where you choose what is visible.
