What this number means

It counts members of Out There Together who have shortlisted or booked at least one upcoming Ambassador Cruise Line sailing. Each person is counted once. It does not count every LGBTQ+ person sailing with Ambassador Cruise Line, so the real number on board will be higher.

We do not rate cruise lines. This page reports what our members are doing, not how welcoming a cruise line is. Out There Together opened to the public in October 2026, so the totals are small and growing. See every cruise line's total.

Frequently asked questions

Are there LGBTQ+ travellers on Ambassador Cruise Line?

Yes. 2 Out There Together members have shortlisted or booked an upcoming Ambassador Cruise Line sailing, as of 21 November 2026. That only counts members of the app, so the real number is higher.

How do I meet other LGBTQ+ travellers on an Ambassador Cruise Line sailing?

Find your Ambassador Cruise Line sailing in the Out There Together app, then add it to your shortlist or mark it Booked. Verified members can see who else is on the same sailing, say hello in the sailing chat and plan meetups before boarding.

Can I book an Ambassador Cruise Line sailing through Out There Together?

No. Out There Together is a social app, not a travel agent. When you are ready to book, we point you to the cruise line or an authorised agent, and some of those partners pay us a referral fee. Details are in our affiliate disclosure.

Does this page show who is on which sailing?

No. Only the total for the cruise line is public. Names, photos, profiles and the sailing each person is on stay inside the app, where you choose what is visible.
