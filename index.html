Frequently asked questions

Do I need to have booked a cruise?

No. Out There Together is for LGBTQ+ adults who have booked a cruise, are still deciding or want to plan with friends. You can mark a sailing as Planning or Booked.

What happens after I join the waitlist?

You will be added to the beta waitlist. Invitations and launch updates will be sent by email as access opens. Joining the waitlist does not give immediate access to the app.
