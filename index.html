Safety

Meet people without giving up control.

  • 18+ and verified

    Every member who sees the community has proved they are over 18 with a photo ID. We keep the result, never the document.

  • You choose what is visible

    Your profile, your photos, your sailing status. Private galleries are friends-only and you can take access back at any time.

  • Report, block, mute

    On every profile, photo and message. A person looks at every report within a day.

  • Know before you go

    Every port shows whether the country protects, restricts or criminalises LGBTQ+ people, as guidance, so you can decide what the day ashore looks like for you.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes, and it stays free. We do not charge members and there are no paid tiers.

How do you make money?

When a member is ready to book, we point them to the cruise line or an authorised agent, and some of those partners pay us a referral fee. We never take a booking or hold your money. Details are in our affiliate disclosure.

Why do I have to verify?

So that everyone who can see who is on board has proved they are an adult. It is what makes people comfortable being themselves here.

Is it a dating app?

No. It is for finding your people on your sailing. Members on a sailing that is under way can opt in to meet one another, and only if both say yes.

Do I need to have booked a cruise?

No. You can mark a sailing as Planning while you decide, or Booked once you have. You can also browse sailings as a guest before creating an account.

Where is it available?

The Android app is on Google Play in the UK, the US and Canada. The iPhone app is with Apple for review and will appear on the App Store once approved. The web version works anywhere.
